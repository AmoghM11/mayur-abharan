The house

A Malleswaram name,
kept deliberately small.

The family has sold jewellery in Malleswaram since 1951. The showroom on 16th Cross carries that work forward — antique gold on one side of the counter, 92.5 silver and silver articles on the other.

What that buys you is attention. Every piece is weighed in front of you, the purity is stamped, the bill is printed, and the person explaining the design is usually the person who chose it. Customers on Justdial and magicpin have rated the showroom 5.0 across 69 ratings.

  • BIS Hallmark showroom. Metal purity is certified and stamped, not claimed.
  • 92.5 sterling, gold polished. The silver is real silver — hallmarked, with antique and gold-polish finishes.
  • Made to order. Customised kada, earrings and gemstone work on request.
  • Weighed in front of you. Printed bill, stated making charge, and exchange terms explained at the counter.

How it works

Chosen, weighed,
stamped, handed over.

No appointment needed and no pressure to buy on the first visit. Most customers come twice — once to look, once to decide.

Start an enquiry WhatsApp us
01

Tell us the occasion

Wedding, naming ceremony, housewarming, a gift, or simply a piece you have wanted for years. Bring a photo or a screenshot — from our Instagram or anywhere else — and we will tell you honestly whether it can be made and what it will weigh.

02

See it in the tray, in daylight

Stock is pulled out and laid on the counter. Nothing is quoted from a catalogue. If what you want is not in the case, customisation is quoted upfront — design, gemstones, size and the making charge, before any work starts.

03

Weighed, hallmarked, billed

The piece is weighed in front of you, the BIS hallmark is shown, and a printed bill goes into the box. Exchange and buy-back terms are explained at the counter so there is nothing to discover later.
