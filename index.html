Our story

75 Years. Three Generations.
One Family.

The story of Sri Manjunatha Jewellers, from a small Malleswaram workshop in 1951 to our new showroom in 2026.

  1. 01 1951Where It All BeganMr Bhaskerrao Shet opens a small workshop at 40/5, 8th Cross.
  2. 02 1986The Family GrowsThe founder’s sons take the business forward.
  3. 03 1996A Husband and Wife Behind the CounterMrs Suchitra Lotlikar joins, running the silver side.
  4. 04 2005Rebuilt With ResilienceA fire, a rebuild, and a grand reopening.
  5. 05 2019A New Generation Takes ResponsibilityMrs Suchitra Lotlikar carries the family business forward.
  6. 06 2024A New ChapterMayur Abharan opens on 16th Cross.
